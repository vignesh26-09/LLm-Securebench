# Evaluator validation plan and current evidence

The automated outcome contract in `core/evaluation/automated_outcome.py` accepts only a structured outcome (`success`, `failure`, `partial`, `ambiguous`, `unavailable`), an explicit reason, evidence, and optional confidence in [0,1]. A confidence number is the evaluator's own report, not a calibrated probability. Parsing/provider failures remain unavailable. This record is labeled `automated_evaluator_unvalidated` and cannot be promoted to human truth.

The human workflow exports blinded samples, preserves a private source join map and source SHA-256, requires two independent raters, and requires an independent adjudicator for every disagreement. `core/evaluation/split_integrity.py` checks unique IDs and prevents declared related groups from crossing development, validation, and test sets. Sampling, related-group assignment, rater training, and semantic near-duplicate review remain human study-design responsibilities.

Evaluate on held-out groups after evaluator prompt/model selection is frozen. Report the full confusion matrix, coverage, raw agreement, accuracy, precision, recall, F1, specificity, balanced accuracy, kappa where meaningful, uncertainty intervals, family strata, and concrete false-positive/false-negative categories. For five-way outcomes, report a separate multiclass table before any predeclared binary mapping. Assess inter-rater agreement before adjudication. Confidence calibration requires held-out human labels and an explicitly predeclared procedure; do not treat self-reported confidence as calibrated.

The existing JailbreakBench artifact contains 300 source records and 295 usable comparisons (98.3% coverage), 53.9% raw agreement, and Cohen's κ approximately 0.211 under its recorded mapping. This low agreement blocks the Gemma judge from replacing independent human labels for target-model safety claims. `core/evaluation/judge_calibration.py` now additionally reports specificity and balanced accuracy for newly built artifacts; the historical artifact is unchanged. No new held-out evaluator validation is claimed.
