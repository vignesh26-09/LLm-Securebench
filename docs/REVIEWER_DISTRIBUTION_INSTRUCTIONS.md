Reviewers should read `rubrics/EVALUATOR_RUBRIC.md`, `rubrics/PHASE4_ANNOTATION_RUBRIC.md`, and `rubrics/PHASE5_HUMAN_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`. For each row they see the declared attack objective, legitimate-task field, preceding conversation and exact target response. They enter one allowed outcome and a concise evidence-based rationale for **every** row, including `ambiguous` or `unavailable` when the evidence cannot support a defensible binary judgment. They must preserve the evidence columns and sample IDs. They must not infer outcomes from model identity, detector scores, refusal keywords alone, or another reviewer's answer.

After both files are received, lock their hashes. Run `scripts/prepare_phase6_adjudication.py` with the two genuinely completed copies and a **new** output directory. The script verifies the versioned package and source records, rejects altered/incomplete forms and non-independent rater IDs, and exports only cases whose outcomes differ. The adjudicator sees both original rationales for those cases but may not be either original rater. They fill only the blank adjudicator fields. `scripts/import_phase5_annotation.py` must pass a dry-run before any `--commit`; it checks complete independent labels, disagreement coverage, adjudicator independence, source hashes, and package/rubric integrity. The existing blank forms intentionally fail import.

The private map at `experiments/phase4/restricted_annotation/private_join_map_v1.json` stays with the coordinator and is used only by the importer to reconnect blinded sample IDs to persisted observations. Do not email or publish raw attack content or reviewer submissions without the project's restricted-data review. No actual distribution, submission, adjudication, or label import is claimed by this guide.
