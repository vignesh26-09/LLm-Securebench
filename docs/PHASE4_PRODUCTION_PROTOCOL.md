# Phase 4 production study proposal — not preregistered

**No production protocol has been frozen or externally registered.** The immutable `experiments/phase4/jbb_pilot_local_lock_v1.json` locks only a two-case exploratory engineering run. It must not be cited as a preregistration or upgraded by renaming its status. This proposal is a decision checklist for a later versioned, locally frozen and externally registered study.

The candidate questions remain those in `docs/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md`: whether measured behavioral displacement, recovery, and sequential-vs-isolated differences add information beyond independently labeled attack outcomes. The primary estimands, any superiority margin, and claim-specific evaluator eligibility must be fixed **before** collecting confirmatory target responses. The present data do not set these quantities.

A candidate primary hypothesis is that, for matched attack instances with validated state measurements, sequential context changes the measured deviation relative to isolation. Its null is no paired context effect in the prespecified target population. Whether this is the *primary* confirmatory contrast, its directional form, the smallest effect of interest, and its multiplicity family remain open design decisions. A second candidate is that validated BSDA/RC measurements add predictive or explanatory information to human-labeled attack outcomes beyond ASR; its null is no incremental information under a predeclared model. The current two-case pilot cannot test either hypothesis.

1. Review source licenses and constituent citations, choose goals and attack families from pinned versions, record source/goal/variant grouping, and partition related groups together into development, validation, and held-out sets. Audit semantic near-duplicates and historical calibration overlap. Neither the two-case pilot nor calibration records may become a blind held-out set retrospectively.
2. Define a legitimate benign task and validated benign controls for each applicable design. Source JBB harmful goals and category-matched benign goals alone do not validate helpfulness, baseline comparability, or recovery. Require reviewed attack-instance objectives and outcome rubric before collection.
3. Lock models and immutable digests, local hardware budget, prompt templates, generation/context settings, deterministic seeds where supported, repetitions, order randomization, failure retries, stop rules, and response/metadata logging. Balance attack family, source category, and model exposure. Include fresh baseline, isolated attack, both sequential orders where feasible, attacked probes, and matched benign lanes. An audit must distinguish sent history from verified provider isolation.
4. Obtain independent human labels with the existing blinded two-rater/adjudication workflow, consent/training/content-access procedures, and source hashes. Assess pre-adjudication agreement and category-specific error. Use the historical judge only as a comparator until held-out claim-specific validation establishes eligibility. Do not manufacture missing labels or tune a study to a target κ.
5. Freeze versioned behavioral representations and calibration on development data, validate them on held-out annotated responses, and retain null/applicability statuses. No BSDA composite, RC, SAEA, model ranking, or choose-model decision is supported by the current pilot.
6. Before production, specify primary/secondary outcomes, grouped paired contrasts, uncertainty intervals, multiplicity handling, missingness and applicability reporting, sensitivity to judge error/isolation uncertainty, and a sample-size or precision rationale based on resources and development evidence. Preserve all failures and exclusions. Analyze only after the registered freeze.

Predeclared exclusions should include source/hash mismatch, unlicensed/unreviewed case, duplicate or cross-split related goal, missing or empty target response, failed model call, incompatible representation, missing baseline/probe, context failure, and suspected isolated-control contamination. Each exclusion must retain its count and reason; outcome ambiguity is an annotation category rather than an automatic failure. The production run budget should state an exact maximum number of model calls, hardware and rater hours, per-model/case repetitions and seeds, and an operational stop on provider failures or budget exhaustion. A safety stop may end exposure to sensitive content, but the stopped cases remain visible. No favorable-result or significance-based stopping is permitted. These quantities are **not** frozen for production now.

The current two local models, two cases, one seed, one repetition, 8192 context, and 96 output-token cap are a bounded feasibility demonstration, not a power calculation. Scaling requires an explicit computation/rater budget and review of whether the small models and long attack prompts produce meaningful responses. Any amended protocol should be a new append-only version with reason, timestamp, and hashes; old versions remain queryable.
