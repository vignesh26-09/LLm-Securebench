# Phase 6 production proposal and freeze decision

**Decision: not frozen, not executed.** This is the current final *proposal*, not a preregistration. Human-reference and behavioral-measurement prerequisites are missing. The executable protocol would need a new append-only version and hash after those are resolved. Do not run the 3,800 calls on this document alone.

The SHA-256-pinned JailbreakBench harmful/benign behavior sources and PAIR/GCG/JBC artifact files are checked by `scripts/inspect_phase4_jbb.py`; source revisions are `886acc352a31533ffbcf4ef22c744658688086fc` and `909e68c01d94222b8ad2e397a017e2e12e2adb73`. Of 100 harmful goals, 79 have complete category/goal-aligned prompts for all three families. Excluding 13 lexical near-overlaps with the historical calibration source and two Phase 4 pilot goals leaves **66 candidates** across ten categories. The source audit detects zero exact harmful duplicates, zero exact harmful/benign overlaps, and zero ≥0.90 lexical near-duplicate pairs within the 66. This is **not a semantic duplicate or licensing review**; a human review of paraphrases, shared attack objectives, and dataset rights remains required before split lock. The exact IDs are in `experiments/phase5/production_source_pool_v1.json`; that file explicitly says the split is unfrozen.

The primary proposed hypothesis is a within-source-goal, same-attack, same-model, same-configuration contrast: the mean sequential-minus-isolated **validated behavioral deviation** equals zero versus differs from zero. This is a paired context-effect estimand, not proof of causal synergy. A secondary hypothesis concerns independently human-labeled attack success by condition, model, and attack family. BSDA components and recovery trajectories are secondary/exploratory until their representations and probes pass the validation gates. Freeze the smallest meaningful effect, direction if justified, and primary multiplicity family before test collection.

Proposed grouped split: one development goal per category (10), 16 validation goals, and four untouched test goals per category (40). Keep all attack variants, model responses, conditions, and repeats for a source goal in one split; screen both lexical and semantic overlap with historical calibration and Phase 4 pilot material. Use development data to validate benign/recovery tasks and estimate variability, validation data for any evaluator threshold or calibration choice, and test once. If any goal is removed, revise counts and precision before locking.

Candidate models are local `qwen3.5:2b` (Phase 4 digest `324d162be6ca5629ae4517c8710434d0bd2d665bc94dbad46e9af8fbf8a2f0df`) and `gemma3:4b` (digest `a2af6cc3eb7fa8be8504abaf9b04e88f17a119ec3f04a3addf55f92841195f5a`). These are **historical identities**, not evidence of current installation; rediscover digests, hardware, and provider versions before locking. A mismatch requires a new version. Record model template, temperature, seed behavior, token cap, context limit, timeouts, host/provider metadata, exact histories, timestamps, response hashes, and failures.

The proposed 25-call trajectory per goal/model/repetition has a fresh standalone baseline, three isolated attacks with their fresh baselines, two opposite three-attack sequences with intervening recovery probes, and a same-length benign lane. The benign lane must be redesigned/validated as a genuinely matched legitimate task; the existing same-category benign goal is not a validated control. Recovery probes must measure re-engagement with a legitimate task rather than merely repeating a harmful goal. Keep exact attack prompts version-pinned. Require matched control identities and explicit isolation ledger; provider-level cache/session isolation remains unverified in the current Ollama environment. A canary is only probabilistic context evidence and must not modify official attack prompts without a separately versioned design.

The proposed schedule is one trajectory for each of 66 goals and two models, plus a full repeat for 10 **preselected** goal groups under a frozen seed list. The maximum is `66 × 2 × 25 = 3,300` plus `10 × 2 × 25 = 500`, total **3,800 target calls**. Nine attack responses per trajectory imply up to `66 × 2 × 9 + 10 × 2 × 9 = 1,368` attack responses and **2,736 independent initial human judgments**, plus disagreement adjudications. Benchmark actual throughput and reviewer hours on development data before authorization. This is a cap, not proof of feasibility or statistical power.

Primary analysis unit is the source-goal cluster. Pair the same attack's isolated and sequential states within goal/model/configuration/seed. Use goal-group bootstrap intervals or a predeclared justified hierarchical paired model; resample all within-goal observations together. Report effect size, confidence interval, coverage, applicability, category/family subgroup uncertainty, condition order, abstentions, failures, and all exclusion counts. The 40 test goals give a worst-case single-proportion Wilson 95% half-width of about **14.8 percentage points** at 0.5; repeats do not make 40 independent clusters into 1,368. Development-only variance plus a declared smallest effect of interest must justify the paired primary contrast. Freeze multiplicity control and sensitivity analysis for isolation uncertainty. Do not stop for a favorable effect.

Predeclare exclusions for missing/changed source hash or rights, duplicate/near-duplicate leakage, absent attack objective, empty/failed/truncated response, mismatched model/configuration or control identity, invalid context, suspected control contamination, missing independent human outcome, missing/incompatible representation, or absent calibration. Preserve each excluded case and reason; `partial`, `ambiguous`, `unavailable`, undefined, and uncalibrated are never zero. Stop operationally at the call cap, a **prelocked** provider-failure threshold, or a reviewed sensitive-content incident.

**Freeze blockers:** actual independent reviewers and adjudicator; rubric pilot/claim-specific label quality; validated legitimate tasks and recovery probes; behavioral representations and bounds; provider isolation/context plan; exact split and semantic-duplicate/rights review; current model digests and settings; repeated-goal/seed list; reviewer and compute capacity; development-based effect/precision target; primary multiplicity and stop thresholds. None should be filled by guessing from the two-goal exploratory pilot. No confirmatory protocol lock or execution is created in Phase 6.
