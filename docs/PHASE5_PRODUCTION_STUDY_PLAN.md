# Phase 5 proposed production study — not frozen or executed

**Status:** executable design proposal with explicit feasibility cap; no confirmatory preregistration or target data collection is authorized by this document. The Phase 4 two-case pilot and its labels, if later obtained, remain exploratory and cannot be reclassified as untouched test evidence. This plan must be amended and hashed **before** any confirmatory response collection, after its measurement and staffing gates pass.

## Research questions and contrasts

Candidate primary contrast: within the same source goal, attack instance, model, and generation configuration, compare validated sequential behavioral deviation with its matched isolated deviation. Null: the population paired context-effect mean is zero. Alternative: it differs from zero. A secondary outcome is independently human-labeled attack success by model, attack family, and condition, with explicit classified denominators. Additional secondary analyses test whether validated BSDA components and recovery trajectories explain human outcomes beyond binary ASR. A positive SAEA delta alone is not causal synergy. Any effect size considered scientifically meaningful, directionality, and multiplicity family must be decided from development evidence before a production lock; the present pilot supplies no validated estimate for that choice.

This is a planning cap, not evidence that the hardware or reviewer team can meet it. Benchmark the actual inference throughput and annotation time on a non-test development subset and secure rater capacity before freeze. No model weights, paid API calls, or production runs are requested now.

## Precision and analysis gate

For a single binary proportion across 40 independent held-out goal clusters, the 95% Wilson interval at a worst-case observed rate near 0.5 has a half-width of approximately **14.8 percentage points**. This is coarse; repeated variants and two reviewers do not create hundreds of independent goal clusters. Exclusions and within-goal correlation can widen intervals. The 40-goal target is a transparent feasibility/precision proposal, **not** a power claim for a small model difference or SAEA effect. Before locking, use development-only variability and a declared smallest effect of interest to calculate paired-effect precision or power; expand or narrow the claim if the cap is insufficient. Do not tune the sample size to a favorable pilot result or target κ.

The primary analysis unit is the source goal, with model/condition/attack as matched within-goal factors. Predeclare the exact estimand and use goal-group bootstrap confidence intervals or a justified hierarchical paired model, preserving the same resampled goal across all conditions. Report per-family/category coverage, missingness, failures, ambiguous/partial human outcomes, and applicability status. Keep human outcomes distinct from automated evaluator predictions. Any evaluator must meet a prelocked, claim-specific held-out error policy before supplying surrogate outcomes. Use prespecified multiplicity control for the primary family and label secondary/exploratory analyses. Report both raw and bounded metric values where the existing code does so; do not convert null values to zero.

## Exclusions, stops, and integrity

Predeclare exclusions for source/license/hash failure, goal-group split leakage, empty/failed responses, missing attack objective, incompatible model/configuration, invalid context history, representation mismatch, absent calibration, and suspected isolated-control contamination. Record every excluded unit and reason. Ambiguous, partial, and unavailable human outcomes remain separate categories. Stop operationally at the 3,800-call cap, a provider-failure threshold fixed from development evidence, or a reviewed sensitive-content incident; never stop for a favorable effect or significance. Save partial runs and missingness. Distinct client conversation IDs and saved histories do not prove provider cache isolation or model attention; `isolation_unverifiable` remains a limitation unless provider-level evidence improves. A canary is probabilistic evidence only and must not silently alter official attack prompts.

**Freeze gate:** exact IDs/splits, source rights, matched benign and recovery tasks, validated human rubric and rater plan, representation/calibration artifacts, effect/precision target, model digests, generation parameters, repeated-goal list, failure-stop threshold, analysis code, and budget must be append-only frozen with a protocol hash. The proposed plan is not preregistered and is not yet eligible for execution as a confirmatory study.
