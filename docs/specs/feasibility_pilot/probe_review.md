# Phase 4 probe-review contract — 2026-10-14

**Status: development-only engineering contract.** No final pilot wording, goal set, probe variant, script, reviewer decision, or adjudication has been approved or created here. The wording used in `tests/test_feasibility_probes.py` is a synthetic software fixture, not a proposed research probe or a human judgment.

Each versioned `ProbeVariant` binds a family, legitimate-task identity, exact wording, rubric version, predecessor hash and amendment reason. `ProbeRegistry` can persist each version as an append-only file. The original version remains independently readable after an amendment. A direct or recognizable indirect request for a protected token is rejected by a conservative lexical gate. That gate cannot establish semantic safety: two actual reviewers must assess authorized purpose, absence of direct or indirect secret solicitation, absence of secret/encoded cues, comparable opportunity to answer normally, and equivalence of task/goal scope while blinded to A/T arm. The reviewers must use distinct identities and source references. Disagreement requires a separate adjudicator with a reason. `ProbeReviewLedger` keeps their records append-only and hash-checked; software cannot authenticate a reviewer's human identity on its own. The research owner must separately approve exact final wording before real execution.

Use held-out development goals and harmless development secrets to check task completion, ambiguity and accidental elicitation. Do not inspect pilot leakage to revise a probe. If a variant changes, issue a new version, redo both reviews, and record adjudication if needed. Freeze variant hashes, rubric, actual reviewer provenance, owner approval and A/T allocation in the later protocol lock. `counterbalanced_variant` alternates two approved variants between A and T within the frozen model/goal/repetition/distance schedule; report realized allocation balance without outcome-dependent switching. The code currently supplies the deterministic scheduling mechanism, not approved variants or an authorized pilot allocation.

The later preflight must reject missing authentic reviews, missing owner approval, missing held-out development validation, or any probe text containing a protected secret. None of these external decisions is represented as completed by Phase 4 mock tests.
