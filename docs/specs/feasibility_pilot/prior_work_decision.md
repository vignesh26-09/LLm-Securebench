# Phase 1 contribution decision (P1.3 / G1)

**Review date:** 2026-10-14. **Classification:** **verified material overlap — G1 STOP.** Do not begin Phase 2 under the current contribution framing. This is a literature-gate decision, **not** a finding that any paper executes the exact seven-arm feasibility design, nor a judgment that the proposed experiment has no value. No C1–C4 claim is promoted to `supported by pilot`; no delayed-secret pilot has been run.

## Why this is the verified-overlap branch

The closest peer-reviewed [medical-injection study](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2842987) already measures an injection's effect on a **later legitimate task** in live six-turn conversations with same-scenario injection/control conditions and a follow-up persistence outcome (Methods, Dialogue Structure; Injection Strategies; Outcome Measures; Results). Its endpoint is unsafe advice, not exact secret disclosure, but it materially overlaps the claim that ordinary later tasks can reveal lasting attack effects under a matched control. [AgentSecBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26269v1) already uses paired benign/adversarial observations, an exact canary-leak event under a legitimate request, and an explicit paired difference/advantage (§§4.3–4.5). [PrivDrift](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30094v2) already intervenes on context distance and measures later secret redisclosure (§§3.2–4). [Agarwal et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-industry.94.pdf) already measures multi-turn system-prompt leakage with first- and second-turn outcomes (§§3–4, 6). These are **material overlaps in constructs and controls**, although spread across different threat models. The contrast table in [P1.2](prior_work_comparison.md) records those differences without turning any unverified detail into an absence claim.

## Decision conditions and limits

This is **not** the “no equivalent method found” branch. Several relevant works have `NV` cells because a publisher abstract is insufficient for a negative method claim; SecReEvalBench's journal full text, MultiBreak/HarmBench, AgentDojo, InjecAgent and StrongREJECT still need full-method trace, and the required independent second reviewer has not signed P1.1–P1.3. These uncertainties cannot restore a broad novelty assertion. They also prevent claiming that the *exact conjunction* is globally original. The present stop is independently warranted by positive, full-method evidence in MED, ASB, PRIV and LEAK; unresolved cells are reported rather than treated as absent.

**Recommended claim reduction for review, without changing the approved hypotheses here:** frame the possible contribution as a *specific controlled measurement comparison* of post-attack exact disclosure during a legitimate task, under live user-history attack, fresh trusted secret, T/R histories and context-valid A–T distances. Attribute delayed legitimate-task effects to MED, active-context secret drift to PRIV, and exact paired marker contrasts to ASB. State that the feasibility study can test whether this conjunction is practical/informative; it cannot claim priority for its ingredients. Any approved narrowing must be versioned through the research decision process, not silently rewritten into `requirements.md` or `design.md` in this phase.

## G1 recommendation

**STOP before Phase 2.** Obtain explicit review of the reduced contribution and an independent source/method audit, especially the `NV` cells closest to the surviving conjunction. If that review finds an exact equivalent, reject or further narrow the affected claim. If it finds no equivalent in adequately reviewed sources, progression may be reconsidered with no global originality claim. No implementation, provider probe or model call is authorized by this document.

**Verification performed:** official publisher/arXiv source inspection and full-method passages cited above; `git diff --check` is the repository formatting check. **Verification not performed:** second-reviewer sign-off, exhaustive/systematic literature search, new model experiments, empirical pilot or scientific validation.
