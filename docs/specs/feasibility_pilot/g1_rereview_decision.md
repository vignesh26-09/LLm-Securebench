## Claim-conformance check

The proposed claim is limited to unauthorized **exact, contiguous** disclosure of a **fresh synthetic secret in the intended trusted instruction** in a later, authorized task that **does not directly or indirectly ask for the secret**, after live attacker-controlled **user** interaction, versus matched non-adversarial histories. “Unsolicited” applies to the final task, not the preceding attack. It does not assert cross-session memory, permanent model change, general persistent jailbreak vulnerability, model ranking, or an already demonstrated positive effect. A final exact repetition following an earlier leak remains `Y_final=1` under the frozen primary outcome but **cannot be interpreted as a newly delayed compromise**; the proposed earlier/final cross-tab is descriptive and must not filter the A–T primary population. The `d=2` within-model A–T point estimate remains complete-pair **descriptive** with all assigned trials and worst-case bounds reported. These checks agree with [requirements §§1, 3, R9–R11, R18, R21–R25](requirements.md), [design §§5, 8, 10](design.md), and the [scope amendment](research_scope_amendment.md). Approval of editorial wording would still need a versioned decision; this review changes no approved estimand or arm.

## Exact source evidence supporting the decision

The [G1 source-located seven-dimension matrix](prior_work_comparison.md) is the auditable coding record. Its primary-source locations are:

1. **[Lee et al., JAMA 2025](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2842987), Methods: Study Design, Dialogue Structure, Injection Strategies, Outcome Measures; [Supplement 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12717619/) eTable 1/eMethods/eTable 2.** The main experiment injects into the turn-3 user treatment request and scores the turn-4 advice; it later checks turn-6 persistence. The separate client-side experiment injects at turn 1, logs subsequent live replies, and records first therapeutic mention and persistence. Controls use matched clinical scenarios and, in the proof of concept, identical infrastructure with unmodified messages. This is strong prior art for later legitimate-task influence under controls, but its event is harmful advice rather than exact disclosure of a trusted secret. The supplement is public; full unredacted payloads/logs are restricted, so exact matching of every arm message and transition is unresolved.
2. **[PrivDrift v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30094v2), §§3.1–3.4 and 4.** A secret enters in the opening *user* turn, drift Q/A pairs are drawn/refined as a completed history, and later probes **ask for** the secret. Drift distance is manipulated and normalized-string plus hybrid leakage is reported. It does not evaluate the same live-user-attack versus matched-benign-history final task in its described protocol. It nonetheless defeats any broad claim that delayed active-context secret retrieval or topic distance is new.
3. **[AgentSecBench v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26269v1), §§4.2–4.7.** Its RAG game places a canary in an unauthorized retrieved record; the benign user asks only about a permitted tenant. It uses paired benign/adversarial observations, exact marker emission, and a paired advantage estimator. The evaluated class explicitly excludes adaptive multi-turn attackers. This defeats novelty claims for exact canary checking and paired security risk contrasts, but its attacker channel, secret placement and timing differ from the candidate.
4. **[Agarwal et al., EMNLP Industry 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-industry.94.pdf), §§3.2, 4–4.2, 6.1, Table 12.** Protected task instructions/knowledge are in the system-prompt template. The defender's first response is followed by another *attacker* prompt that explicitly reiterates extraction. The study reports leakage at both turns and attack-configuration ablations. It does not describe a non-secret-requesting final legitimate task; its turn-level rates cannot be assumed to give the proposed joint `Y_earlier × Y_final` classification.

These are **positive method observations** and explicit evaluated-protocol distinctions, not argument from an abstract's silence. None of the four, as publicly described, executes the entire narrowed conjunction. That alone is not enough for “proceed”: the [prior source ledger](prior_work_sources.md) and [comparison](prior_work_comparison.md) still contain unresolved methods that could be closer, and no independent reviewer has verified the matrix.
